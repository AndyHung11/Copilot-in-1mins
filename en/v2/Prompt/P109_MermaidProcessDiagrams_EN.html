💡 Three Ways to Use It
🧜 Know what Mermaid is
Mermaid is a text-based language for describing processes; Copilot turns the text into a diagram you can see and share.
📎 Add your own files
Attach your process documents or SOPs so Copilot draws your real process, not a generic one.
🛠️ Fixing the ( ) error
Copilot sometimes inserts ( ) into the code, which Mermaid cannot handle; choose “Edit in Pages,” view the code, and delete the parentheses.
