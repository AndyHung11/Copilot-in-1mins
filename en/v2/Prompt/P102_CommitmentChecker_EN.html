🚀 Copy This Prompt (English Version)
🤝 Prompt of the Week (turn on WorkIQ first, then paste into Copilot Chat — Work)

Review my emails, Teams chats and meeting recaps from the last 7 days and identify every open commitment. Group them into two lists: Others → Me: things other people committed to deliver to me. Me → Others: things I committed to deliver to other people. For each item show: who, what exactly was promised, the due date (if none was mentioned, write "no date"), the source (email subject, chat or meeting name) and a status (Open / Overdue / Unclear). Leave out anything that has clearly been delivered or closed since. Finish with the three items that need my attention first and suggest a one-line follow-up message for each.

Try it in: Copilot Chat – Work.

🤝 本週 Prompt（先開啟 WorkIQ，再貼進 Copilot Chat – Work）

檢視我過去 7 天的電子郵件、Teams 聊天與會議摘要，找出所有尚未完成的承諾。請分成兩份清單：別人 → 我：別人答應要交付給我的事項。我 → 別人：我答應要交付給別人的事項。每一筆請列出：是誰、確切承諾了什麼、到期日（若未提及，請寫「無日期」）、出處（信件主旨、聊天或會議名稱），以及狀態（進行中／逾期／不明確）。請排除在那之後已明確交付或結案的項目。最後列出最需要我優先處理的三件事，並為每一件建議一句跟催訊息。

可在：Copilot Chat – Work 中使用。
💡 Three Ways to Use It
1
🔓 Turn on WorkIQ first
This prompt reads organizational data (email, Teams, meeting recaps), so WorkIQ (formerly Work mode) must be enabled in Copilot Chat or you'll get a generic answer with nothing behind it.
2
⏰ Put it on a schedule
Friday 3pm and Monday 8am both work well: one closes the week, the other sets it up. Scheduling it means you never have to remember.
3
🔍 Watch the “Unclear” entries
Those are the ones it couldn't confidently call a commitment, which is often exactly where two people remember the conversation differently. Worth checking yourself.
