🚀 Copy This Prompt (English Version)
Using my top 5 goals for [YEAR]: [GOAL 1], [GOAL 2], [GOAL 3], [GOAL 4], [GOAL 5], create a postcard-style image addressed to Future Me.
Include:
- A title ‘Dear Future Me’
- My 5 goals presented visually with icons, short phrases, or bullet points
Use fun, uplifting colors or design elements. It’s equal parts inspiration, intention-setting, and desk-worthy décor.
📋 提示詞範本（繁體中文版）
請根據我 [年份] 年的前 5 大目標：[目標 1]、[目標 2]、[目標 3]、[目標 4]、[目標 5]，製作一張寫給「未來的我」的明信片風格圖片。
內容包含：
- 標題「Dear Future Me」
- 以圖示、簡短句子或重點條列，視覺化呈現我的 5 個目標
請使用有趣、讓人振奮的色彩或設計元素。它要同時是一份鼓舞、一次心願設定，也是一件適合擺在桌上的裝飾。
💡 Three Ways to Use It
🎯 Have your five goals ready
Replace [YEAR] and [GOAL 1]–[GOAL 5] with your own; one short sentence per goal keeps the text on the image readable.
🖼️ Paste it into Create
Paste the prompt into Create in Copilot; if you already have a written vision document, use Add content to bring it in.
🔁 Refine until it feels right
Ask for a different palette, layout, or icon style until it is something you want on your desk, then share it with colleagues if you like.
