📆 Predict my workload (paste into Copilot Chat)

Based on the last 60 days of emails, predict my workload for next month including: 1) Expected email volume by week, 2) Likely deadlines and time commitments, 3) Task clusters (related work items), 4) High-pressure weeks (crunch times), 5) Meetings and collaborative work load, 6) Personal bandwidth vs. expected demand. Suggest pacing strategy and workflow optimization recommendations to balance load.
📋 提示詞範本（繁體中文版）
🔓 先開啟 WorkIQ — 這個 Prompt 要讀你近 60 天的信件（組織資料），務必在 Copilot Chat 開啟 WorkIQ（前稱 Work 模式），否則只會得到空泛內容。

📆 預測下個月的工作負荷（貼進 Copilot Chat）

根據近 60 天的信件，預測我下個月的工作負荷，包括：1) 每週預期信件量，2) 可能的截止日與時間承諾，3) 任務群集（相關的工作項），4) 高壓週（硬仗期），5) 會議與協作負荷，6) 個人頻寬 vs. 預期需求。並請提出排節奏策略與工作流程優化建議，以平衡負荷。
💡 Three Ways to Use It
1
🔓 Turn on WorkIQ first
This prompt reads your email and other organizational data, so enable WorkIQ (formerly Work mode) in Copilot Chat and make sure you have an M365 Copilot license, or you'll only get generic output.
2
📅 Cross-check with your calendar
This forecast is based on email patterns alone; cross-reference it with your calendar and known timelines to confirm the crunch weeks.
3
🔁 Run it every month
Run it at the start or end of the month so you pace ahead instead of reacting after the fact.
