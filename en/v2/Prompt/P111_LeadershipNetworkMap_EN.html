🚀 Copy This Prompt (English Version)
Act as an executive coach specializing in leadership influence and organizational networks. Using my Microsoft 365 activity data (Teams messages, Outlook emails, meeting participation) and, if available, contextual LinkedIn profile information, analyze my professional network over the past 3 months.

Objectives
- Assess the strength, structure, and effectiveness of my leadership network.
- Identify risks related to over‑reliance, limited visibility, or constrained influence.
- Provide actionable recommendations aligned to executive growth.

Analysis Steps
Key Network Nodes
Identify up to 25–30 influential people I interact with most. Classify each as:
- Collaborators (execution and delivery)
- Sponsors/Promoters (visibility and advocacy)
- Mentors/Advisors (development and guidance)
- Connectors (bridging teams, orgs, or external networks)

Tie Strength
Evaluate each relationship as strong, moderate, or weak based on interaction frequency, reciprocity, and channel diversity (Teams, email, meetings).

Network Structure
- Identify clusters, silos, and bridging individuals.
- Highlight where relationships are redundant or overly concentrated.

Executive Network Assessment
- Effective size: Are the same people serving multiple roles?
- Density: Is my network tightly clustered or broadly distributed?
- Constraint: Am I overly dependent on a small group?
- Heterogeneity: Diversity of function, seniority, organization, and perspective.

Coaching Insights
- Key strengths of my current network.
- Gaps limiting influence, scale, or advancement.
- Risks to resilience or succession readiness.

Actionable Coaching Recommendations
5–7 concrete actions to strengthen sponsorship, expand cross‑org influence, and diversify weak ties over the next 6–12 months.

Output Requirements
- Executive‑level summary (clear, concise, non‑academic).
- Table of key relationships with role and tie strength.
- Bullet‑point coaching insights and recommended actions.
- Base conclusions only on observable interaction patterns; avoid speculation.
📋 提示詞範本（繁體中文版）
請扮演專精領導影響力與組織人脈的高階主管教練。請根據我的 Microsoft 365 活動資料（Teams 訊息、Outlook 信件、會議參與），以及（若可取得）LinkedIn 個人檔案的背景資訊，分析我過去 3 個月的職場人脈。

目標
- 評估我的領導人脈的強度、結構與成效。
- 找出過度依賴、能見度不足或影響力受限的風險。
- 提供與高階主管成長方向一致、可執行的建議。

分析步驟

關鍵人脈節點
找出我互動最頻繁、最具影響力的最多 25～30 人，並將每個人分類為：
- 協作者（負責執行與交付）
- 支持者／推手（提升能見度、為我發聲）
- 導師／顧問（提供發展與指引）
- 串連者（串起團隊、組織或外部網絡）

關係強度
依互動頻率、互惠程度與管道多樣性（Teams、信件、會議），將每段關係評估為強、中或弱。

人脈結構
- 找出群集、孤島與橋接者。
- 標示關係重複或過度集中的地方。

高階人脈評估
- 有效規模：是否由同一批人扮演多種角色？
- 密度：我的人脈是緊密聚集，還是廣泛分布？
- 限制：我是否過度依賴一小群人？
- 異質性：職能、資歷、組織與觀點的多元程度。

教練洞察
- 目前人脈的主要優勢。
- 限制影響力、規模或晉升的缺口。
- 對韌性或接班準備度的風險。

可執行的教練建議
提出 5～7 項具體行動，在未來 6～12 個月內強化支持者關係、擴大跨組織影響力，並讓弱連結更多元。

輸出要求
- 高階主管層級的摘要（清楚、精簡、不學術）。
- 關鍵關係表，包含角色與關係強度。
- 以重點條列呈現教練洞察與建議行動。
- 結論僅依據可觀察的互動模式，避免臆測。
