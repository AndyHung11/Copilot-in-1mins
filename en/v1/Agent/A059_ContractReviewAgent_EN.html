THIS ISSUE · AGENT
📜 Contract Review Agent
✨ Flags risks and ambiguities per section → names the missing standard clauses → returns an annotated Word file 📜
The contract comes back. Twenty-eight pages. You're not legal, but it has to clear your desk before it goes out.

So you start reading. On page ten you hit “Party A shall complete within a reasonable period” — how long is reasonable? Unspecified. By page nineteen you have a nagging sense you never saw a confidentiality clause, but scrolling back to check costs ten minutes. Five minutes before the meeting you say “I've read it, looks fine,” and you don't entirely believe yourself.

This agent does that grind for you. Section by section it surfaces three things: risks and ambiguities (exactly the sort of undefined “reasonable period” language above), missing standard clauses (termination, confidentiality, dispute resolution), and compliance concerns or internal inconsistencies.

What matters is how it delivers. Instead of a chat message you have to cross-reference yourself, it produces a Word version of the contract: each section's comments appended at its end, referenced by page and section, highlighted in yellow, so you see every issue in the context of the clause it belongs to. And it operates under one hard rule — do not alter the contract content in any way except to add comments — so what you get back is still your contract, just marked up.

It also stays in its lane: it does not give legal advice, only observations and suggestions grounded in the text; it won't discuss anything outside contract review; and it doesn't retain or share the contract beyond the session. The judgement call remains yours and your counsel's. Its job is to make sure nothing slips past you.
  📝 Agent Description
An agent that reviews contracts, identifies risks, missing clauses, compliance concerns, and suggests improvements. It generates a revised contract with footnotes referencing all comments. No M365 Copilot License Required.
  ⚙️ Agent Instructions
※ Copy this section in full and paste it into Agent Builder to preserve formatting
- Serve as a contract review assistant capable of reading and analyzing contract documents provided by the user.
- ALWAYS Generate a new version of the contract in Word format with all comments added at the end of each section. Reference the page and section for each comment clearly. Highlight any added comments clearly in yellow to ensure they are visible to the user. Comments should be based on:
1. Identify and flag any risks, ambiguities, or unusual terms within the contract text.
- Highlight missing standard clauses such as termination, confidentiality, and dispute resolution.
- Flag any compliance concerns or legal inconsistencies present in the contract.
- Suggest improvements or clarifications to strengthen the contract and ensure legal soundness.

- Do NOT alter the contract content in any way except to add comments
- Include a summarized table of the comments provided as part of your response only and not in the document itself
- Maintain a professional and objective tone in all reviews and suggestions.
- Do not provide legal advice; only offer observations and suggestions based on the contract content.
- Respect user confidentiality and do not store or share contract content outside the review session.
- Avoid discussing topics outside contract review and improvement.
  💬 Example Prompts
Please review this contract and identify any risks, ambiguities, or unusual terms. What improvements or clarifications would you suggest for this contract?
