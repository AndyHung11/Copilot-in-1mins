THIS ISSUE · PROMPT
🔬 Document Comparison - Deep Review
✨ Present／Missing／Modified per section → audits obligations and SLAs → three-tier risk ranking 🔬
Most of the time you just want to know what changed between two versions — and for that, P099 is enough: fast, clean, done.

But some documents can't be reviewed on “what's different” alone. A supplier returns a revised contract. A Tier-1 spec has to be checked against OEM requirements. SLAs and compliance clauses need validating before SOP. What actually hurts you there isn't changed wording — it's an obligation quietly removed, an SLA that slid from 99.9% to 99.5%, a termination clause that gained a new unilateral exit for the other side. Those hide inside sections that exist in both versions, so presence-checking alone will never surface them.

This version adds four safeguards. First, section status moves from “in A / in B / in both” to Present／Missing／Modified — changed sections announce themselves instead of waiting to be found in the differences column. Second, it no longer leaves “substantive difference” to the model's judgement; it supplies a named checklist: contradictions, conflicts, new or removed obligations, requirements, constraints, SLAs, termination terms, and compliance statements. Third, the no-inference rule widens from “don't read meaning into formatting” to don't infer intent or business rationale, and it explicitly forbids summarizing or rewriting — the moment a clause gets summarized, legal precision is gone. Fourth, ranking goes from two tiers to Critical → Medium → Minor, so the middle ground stops getting flattened.

The cost is a longer run and a longer output. So pick by situation: routine version diffs go to P099; anything carrying obligations or risk comes here.
  📋 Prompt Template (English)
🔬 Prompt of the Week (upload both documents first, then paste)

Analyze and systematically compare Document A and Document B.

Instructions:
Identify the document type for each (contract, technical specification, policy, report).
Extract explicit sections and headings from each document.
Compare explicit text content only. Do not infer intent, meaning, or business rationale.
Ignore formatting differences.
For each section, determine: Present / Missing / Modified.
Highlight substantive differences, including contradictions, conflicts, new or removed obligations, requirements, constraints, SLAs, termination terms, or compliance statements.
If a difference is unclear or ambiguous, flag: "Requires human review."

Output format: Structured table with columns: Section | Doc A Status | Doc B Status | Key Differences

Constraints:
– Focus strictly on explicit text.
– Do not summarize or rewrite documents.
– Rank findings by importance: Critical → Medium → Minor.

Try it in: Copilot Chat – Work, or Copilot in Word.
  📋 提示詞範本（繁體中文版）
🔬 本週 Prompt（先上傳兩份文件，再貼上）

分析並系統化比較文件 A 與文件 B。

指示：
辨識每一份文件的類型（合約、技術規格、政策、報告）。
從每一份文件中擷取明確的章節與標題。
僅比較明確存在的文字內容。不要推論意圖、語意或商業理由。
忽略排版格式上的差異。
針對每一個章節，判定：Present（存在）／ Missing（缺少）／ Modified（已修改）。
標示實質差異，包括矛盾、衝突，以及新增或移除的義務、要求、限制、SLA、終止條款或法遵聲明。
若某項差異不清楚或有歧義，標記：「Requires human review。」

輸出格式：結構化表格，欄位為：章節 | 文件 A 狀態 | 文件 B 狀態 | 主要差異

限制：
－ 嚴格聚焦於明確存在的文字。
－ 不要摘要或改寫文件內容。
－ 依重要性排序：Critical → Medium → Minor。

可在：Copilot Chat（Work）或 Word 中的 Copilot 使用。
💡 Usage Tips
1. 🪶 Only need to know what changed? Use P099 — This version runs longer and outputs more because it audits obligations and risk. For plain version diffs (decks, reports, draft specs), P099 is the faster call.
2. 🎯 Name your red lines up front — Add “pay particular attention to payment terms, liability caps, and data protection clauses” and the three-tier ranking will track your actual risk posture.
3. 🔍 Read Modified and “Requires human review” first — The first means the section survived but its content moved; the second is the model admitting it can't call this one. The highest-risk clauses almost always sit in those two buckets.
