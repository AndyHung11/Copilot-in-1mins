THIS ISSUE · PROMPT
🧳 IRL Collab Tour
🧳 Scan six weeks of email, calendar, and Teams → find collaborators based there → list their contact details → book the catch-ups before you land
You have a trip next week and a packed agenda. Then it hits you: who do you actually work with in that city? Digging through your inbox, Teams, and calendar eats half an hour.

All too often you only realize it on arrival—after missing the coffee chat that could have settled a long-running thread in person.

This prompt asks Copilot to scan your last six weeks of email, calendar, and Teams messages, find the people based at your destination you have genuinely collaborated with, and list their contact details. Swap in the location, spend one minute before you leave, and turn digital collaboration into real-life catch-ups.
  📋 Prompt Template (English)
I’m heading to the Microsoft campus in Redmond. Check my email, calendar, and Teams messages from the last 6 weeks. Tell who I’ve collaborated with that are based in Redmond. List their contact information so I can reach out to them to let them know I’m coming.
  📋 提示詞範本（繁體中文版）
我要去 Redmond 的 Microsoft 園區。請查看我過去 6 週的信件、行事曆和 Teams 訊息，告訴我有哪些和我合作過的人位於 Redmond，並列出他們的聯絡資訊，讓我能先聯絡他們，告知我即將到訪。
💡 Usage Tips
🔓 Turn on WorkIQ first This prompt reads your email, calendar, and Teams messages, so enable WorkIQ (formerly Work mode) in Copilot Chat.
📍 Swap in your destination Replace both mentions of Redmond with your city or office, and adjust the six-week look-back if you like.
✅ Double-check before reaching out Copilot infers location from your interactions, so confirm the person will actually be there before you propose a meeting.
