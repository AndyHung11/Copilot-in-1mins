🍭 PowerPoint Brand Kit Deck Builder
✨ Upload brand assets → build your kit → generate on-brand decks in one line 🎁
Every time you build a deck, do you find yourself digging up the brand guidelines PDF again, manually matching colors, fonts, and logos slide by slide — only to realize halfway through you used the wrong shade and have to start over?

Copilot Brand Kit lets you upload all of that once and package it into a kit: templates, fonts, logos, colors, images, even your brand voice. Already have a brand guidelines PDF? Upload it and you'll have a ready-to-use kit in seconds, shareable with your whole team. Beyond kits you build yourself, there's also an official Microsoft brand kit and a Zava demo kit to try.

From there, whether you pick a kit inside Copilot Create or fly over to PowerPoint, click the Copilot icon, hit the "+" sign, select brand, and prompt something like "create a deck from this outline" — the slides come out already dressed in your brand's colors and style. No more manual slide-by-slide fixes.

🍭 AI Tip of the Week (Eva Etchells, Viva Engage)

Sugar, spice, and a nice branded PowerPoint

Creating branded PowerPoint decks? Copilot Brand Kit features are not just cute, they're powerful too.

Start in Create in Copilot > More… > Brand kits.

Create your own kit by uploading your brand ingredients like templates, fonts, logos, colors, images, voice. If you already have brand guidelines in a PDF, upload that and have something ready to use in seconds and share your kits with others. There's also official Microsoft brand and Zava kits.

Use kits in Copilot Create or fly over to PowerPoint and select the Copilot icon, in the prompt box, smash that + sign > select brand, and choose the kit you want to use. Prompt something like "create a deck from this [insert outline]".

And so once again the day is saved, thanks to PowerPoint. 🍭

Try it in: Copilot Create or PowerPoint.

🍭 本週 AI 小撇步（Eva Etchells，Viva Engage）

甜蜜、辛香，再加一份有品牌感的 PowerPoint

要做有品牌風格的 PowerPoint 簡報嗎？Copilot Brand Kit 功能不只可愛，還很實用。

從 Copilot 的「建立」> 更多… > 品牌套件 開始。

把你的品牌素材——範本、字型、Logo、色票、圖片、語氣——通通上傳，打造你自己的套件。如果你已經有一份品牌規範 PDF，直接上傳，幾秒鐘就能做好一個可用的套件，還能分享給其他人。系統也內建了官方 Microsoft 品牌套件與 Zava 套件可以試用。

你可以在 Copilot Create 裡選套件使用，或直接飛到 PowerPoint，點選 Copilot 圖示，在提示框裡按下「+」號 > 選擇品牌 > 挑你要用的套件。接著打一句像是「根據這份大綱做一份簡報」的提示。

於是，PowerPoint 又一次拯救了你的一天。🍭

可在：Copilot Create 或 PowerPoint 中使用。
💡 Usage Tips
1. 📎 Got brand guidelines as a PDF? — Upload it directly, faster than typing every color code and font by hand.
2. 🤝 Share the kit with your team — once it's built, so everyone's decks look consistent.
3. 🧪 Want to try it first? — Use the built-in Microsoft brand or Zava kit to get the hang of it.
