THIS ISSUE · PROMPT
🏅 Leveled-Up Learning Badges
✨ Newbie → Adventurer → Legend — turn learning into a quest 🎮
You finish a course or wrap a project, and all that's left is a deck you'll forget by Friday. No medal, no milestone—the effort dries up like a water stain.

This prompt turns learning into a level-up quest: straight in Copilot's Create tab, it generates a 🌱Newbie → ⚔️Adventurer → 🏆Legend badge set in one go. All three share the same design skeleton and outline; only the color and icon rank up—fresh to dazzling, plain to ornate—so the row reads as one collectible achievement system.

No Illustrator, no color swatches, no waiting on a designer. Swap [Your Team or Course Name] for your program and remix the tiers however you like ([Padawan]/[Knight]/[Master]?), and minutes later you've got a glow-up of ceremony that looks great in Teams shoutouts, decks, or a learning page.
  📋 Prompt Template (English)
🎮 Level-Up Quest Badges

Design a set of "level-up / quest" style badges with three ranks, going from weakest to strongest: 🌱 [Newbie], ⚔️ [Adventurer], 🏆 [Legend] — capturing a fun zero-to-hero progression.

The three badges should feel like one game achievement system: the same outline shape and overall design, each with the program name [Your Team or Course Name] across the top. As the rank climbs, make the palette richer and the icon fancier (e.g. Newbie fresh and light, Adventurer bold and fiery, Legend dazzling and glowing), while keeping one consistent style so they clearly read as a series.

Place the three badges side by side, without overlapping, each with a full, uncropped edge, clean and ready to hand out as awards.
  📋 提示詞範本（繁體中文版）
🎮 學習闖關徽章三連發

幫我設計一張「升級打怪」風格的徽章圖，包含三個闖關等級，由弱到強分別是 🌱 [新手]、⚔️ [勇者]、🏆 [傳說]，呈現一種「從菜鳥一路打到大神」的成長感。

三顆徽章要像同一套遊戲成就系統：相同的外框形狀與整體設計，每顆上方都放上活動名稱 [你的團隊／課程名稱]。隨著等級越高，配色越濃烈、圖示越華麗（例如新手清新、勇者熱血、傳說閃耀奪目），但整體風格維持一致、一看就是一系列。

請讓三顆徽章並排、彼此不重疊，每顆都有完整邊緣不被裁切，整體乾淨好看、可直接當獎章使用。
💡 Usage Tips
1
🎨 Paste in Create & rename
In Copilot → Create tab, paste the full prompt and swap [Your Team or Course Name] for your program; the three tiers are yours to remix too (e.g. [Newbie]/[Adventurer]/[Legend], or [Padawan]/[Knight]/[Master]).
2
🔧 Tweak a single badge
Want the top tier to feel epic? Reply 'make the Legend badge blazing gold with a laurel wreath'—refine just that one without redrawing the set.
3
📥 Download transparent & drop in
Save the transparent PNG and paste it into Teams shoutouts, deck covers, or a SharePoint learning page—instant award-ceremony vibes.
