# Equipment Anomaly Report (Fictional Training Sample)

> A line supervisor describes an equipment anomaly and hands it to Copilot Cowork so it can handle two outbound / cross-system actions at once: drafting an email to the maintenance vendor, and scheduling an on-site inspection meeting. Both require your approval before anything is actually sent or created.

## Report Details
- Reported by: Wang Xiaoming (Line B supervisor)
- Equipment ID: CNC-B-07 (5-axis machining center)
- Time observed: 2026-07-28 14:20
- Anomaly description: Abnormal spindle vibration and noise during machining; dimensional tolerance exceeded the upper limit (+0.05mm); 3 consecutive parts have been judged defective.
- Current status: Taken out of service; on-site staff could not resolve it.
- Actions already tried: Re-calibrated the tooling and replaced cutting fluid; noise and vibration were unchanged.
- Impact assessment: This machine accounts for about 35% of Line B's daily output; continued downtime is expected to affect the shipping schedule for today's order XYZ-2207.
- Warranty status: The last major overhaul was in March 2026; the machine is still under warranty.

## Two Things Requested from Cowork
1. **Draft an email to the maintenance vendor**: Huyi Precision Equipment Service, contact Engineer Chen (chen.eng@example-vendor.com). The email should describe the equipment ID, symptoms, actions already tried, and warranty status, and request an urgent on-site inspection of the spindle bearing, including an internal emergency contact.
2. **Schedule an on-site inspection meeting**: invite the QA manager and the equipment engineering team, ideally within the next two days to coincide with the vendor's visit, so internal staff and the vendor can jointly confirm the root cause instead of trading information back and forth.

## Note
- This is a fictional training case; names, vendors, and email addresses are placeholders — replace them with your own real contacts before actual use.
