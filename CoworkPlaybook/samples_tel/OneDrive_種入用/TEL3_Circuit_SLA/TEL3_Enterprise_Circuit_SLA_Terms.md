# Enterprise Circuit SLA — Contract Terms (extract)

> Document owner: Network Engineering　|　CKT-88107　|　Revision 2.4

## Clause 7 — Service Level

7.1 The Supplier (Zava Telecom) guarantees monthly availability of **99.9%** for this
circuit. Monthly availability = (total minutes in month − counted outage minutes)
÷ total minutes in month × 100%.

7.2 For a 31-day month, the monthly allowance is **44.6 minutes**.

## Clause 8 — What counts as an outage

8.1 The following are **excluded** from outage time:

　(a) **Planned maintenance** — work notified to the Customer in writing seven days
　　　in advance and acknowledged.
　(b) **Customer premises equipment** — interruption caused by the Customer's own
　　　equipment, cabling or power.
　(c) **Force majeure** — natural disaster, war, act of government and other causes
　　　not attributable to the Supplier.

8.2 All other interruptions are counted as outage time.

⚠️ The three exclusions in 8.1 often leave a large gap between total interruption
　 and counted outage. **A breach must be assessed on counted outage time, never on
　 total interruption.**

## Clause 9 — Guaranteed Window

9.1 The guaranteed window is **Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00**.

9.2 Counted outage falling within the guaranteed window is subject to a separate
　 threshold of **15 minutes**, **assessed independently of the monthly
　 allowance in Clause 7**.

9.3 Compensation for outage within the guaranteed window is payable at **3×**
　 the amount calculated under Clause 10.

⚠️ **Clause 9.2 is an independent threshold.** Even where counted outage for the
　 month is within the Clause 7.2 allowance, the Supplier remains liable if counted
　 outage within the guaranteed window exceeds this threshold.

## Clause 10 — Compensation

10.1 Compensation is calculated on the **minutes in excess of the threshold**, as a
　　percentage of the monthly charge:

| Minutes over threshold | Compensation (% of monthly charge) |
|---|---|
| More than 0 | 5% |
| More than 10 | 10% |
| More than 30 | 20% |

10.2 The monthly charge for this circuit is **NTD 86,000**.

10.3 Compensation is applied as a credit against the following month's charges.

## Clause 11 — Time limit

11.1 A claim must be made within thirty days of the end of the month concerned.
