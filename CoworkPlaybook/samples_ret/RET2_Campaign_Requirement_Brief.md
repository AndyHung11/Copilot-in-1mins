# Campaign Requirement Brief: Autumn 2026 "Warmth of Home" Living Festival

> Fictional training sample; brand, stores, suppliers and figures are placeholders.

## 1. Basics

- **Campaign name**: Autumn 2026 "Warmth of Home" Living Festival
- **Campaign window**: 2026/09/18 (Fri) - 2026/10/15 (Thu), 28 days
- **Requesting team**: Merchandising & Campaign Planning
- **Participating channels**: 8 stores nationwide + own online store + Marketplace A/B + live-commerce

## 2. Campaign targets

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Omnichannel revenue | +18% versus the same period last year |
| New members | +3,000 |
| Average ticket in hero categories | +12% |
| Overall campaign gross margin | Must not fall below 32% |

## 3. Hero categories and strategic direction

1. **Home textiles**: the seasonal driver - build around a "refresh your bedding for autumn" story and lift the average
   ticket with bundle pricing.
2. **Kitchen & cooking**: the highest average-ticket category - suitable as the threshold driver for spend-and-get gifts.
3. **Fragrance & lighting**: low price point, high repeat rate - suitable as a traffic and add-on driver to raise
   attachment rate.

## 4. Budget

- Marketing spend: NT$1,800,000 (online media, in-store display, printed flyers)
- Gift-with-purchase cost: NT$600,000

## 5. Deliverables required (all prepared in one pass)

1. **Campaign plan** (Word): theme rationale, targets, category strategy, discount mechanics, channel responsibilities,
   timeline, risks and contingencies.
2. **SKU discount and margin model** (Excel): per-SKU discounted price, post-discount gross margin, forecast units and
   campaign gross profit, verifying that the overall margin holds at 32%.
3. **Store execution SOP** (PDF, one page for the back-of-house board): display priorities, gift-issuing rules, POS
   handling notes, and responses to common customer complaints.
4. **Supplier notification email draft**: campaign window, required stock build and delivery deadline, gift supply
   requirements - formal in tone but not heavy-handed.

## 6. Constraints (must be respected)

- Overall campaign gross margin **must not fall below 32%**; verify the math before proposing any discount.
- The gift threshold must be a single amount the POS can recognize (for example "spend NT$2,000 in one transaction");
  it must not require manual judgement.
- SKUs with on-hand stock below 300 units must not be hero items, to avoid stock-outs mid-campaign.
- The discounted price must not fall below 1.25x cost.
