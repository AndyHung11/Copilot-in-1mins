# Pricing Rules and Competitive Research Brief

> Fictional training sample; brand, SKUs and figures are placeholders.

## 1. Purpose of this research

Ahead of the autumn campaign we need a view of publicly available pricing and promotional practice for comparable home
and living goods, as the basis for deciding whether to reprice our key SKUs. The point is not to copy competitor prices,
but to answer: **which SKUs are clearly overpriced, which have room to go up, and which should be defended with bundles
rather than discounts.**

## 2. External intelligence required

1. **Publicly published promotional practice** over the last three months for comparable home goods (bedding, cookware,
   fragrance and lighting): typical discount depth, spend-and-get thresholds, and bundle formats.
2. The marketing themes and campaign lengths that major channels typically use for the **autumn season-change window**.
3. The **publicly listed retail price range** for comparable products (verifiable public sources only, with links).
4. Recent **consumer discussion themes** in the home-goods category (for example sustainable materials, storage, gifting).

## 3. Pricing rules (hard limits, must not be broken)

| ID | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| R-01 | Post-change gross margin **must not fall below 38%**. Anything below that line is not discounted; respond with bundles or gifts instead. |
| R-02 | A single price reduction must not exceed **15%** of the current price, to protect the brand's price-band perception. |
| R-03 | SKUs selling fewer than 100 units in the last four weeks **must not be discounted as the first move**; review display and exposure first. |
| R-04 | SKUs with gross margin above 55% that rank in the top three by units may be assessed for a **small increase** (3%-5%). |
| R-05 | Every pricing recommendation must state the resulting gross margin and the estimated change in gross profit. "Recommend a discount" alone is not acceptable. |

## 4. Deliverable format

Extend the key-SKU baseline list with the following columns and output as Excel:

- Competitor reference price range (with source)
- Our relative position (high / in line / low)
- Recommended action (reduce / hold / increase / switch to bundle)
- Recommended new price
- Resulting gross margin
- Rationale (one sentence, referencing a rule ID from the table above)

Close with an "overall recommendation summary" stating how many SKUs are proposed for change, the estimated impact on
overall gross margin, and which SKUs should be left alone in favor of other levers.

## 5. Citation requirement

External findings must include clickable source links. If no reliable public source can be found for a data point, state
"no reliable public source found" explicitly - **do not fill the gap with an estimated number.**
