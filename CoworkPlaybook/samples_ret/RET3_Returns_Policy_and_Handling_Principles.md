# Returns Policy and Complaint Handling Principles (Customer Service internal)

> Fictional training sample; the clauses are written for teaching purposes and are not a real legal document.

## 1. Returns and exchange policy

| ID | Clause |
| --- | --- |
| P-01 | **Online channels** (own store / marketplaces / live-commerce): a seven-day inspection period starting the day after delivery, during which returns may be requested without cause, provided packaging and accessories are intact. |
| P-02 | **Physical stores**: non-defective returns are handled as an exchange or equivalent-value item, within seven days of purchase, with the receipt and intact packaging. |
| P-03 | **Defective goods** (all channels): within fourteen days of delivery or purchase, once confirmed as a manufacturing defect, the customer may choose a full refund or a replacement, and **the company pays return shipping**. |
| P-04 | **Late delivery**: if the order has not shipped three business days beyond the published delivery window, the customer may cancel for a full refund, independent of the inspection period. |
| P-05 | Intimate apparel, food, and opened personal hygiene products are excluded from the inspection period, except where defective. |
| P-06 | Refund timing: within three to five business days of receiving the returned item; credit-card reversals follow the issuing bank's schedule. |

## 2. Case severity and response times

| Level | Criteria | First response | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A (high) | Defect with a safety concern, customer explicitly threatens escalation or public complaint, or no reply for over 48 hours | Within 4 hours | Requires supervisor review |
| B (medium) | Ordinary defective return/exchange, late delivery, amount disputes | Within 1 business day | Agent may handle |
| C (low) | Product questions, policy clarification, uncontested returns | Within 2 business days | Agent may handle |

## 3. Reply-writing principles

1. **Apologize, explain, then give a clear next step** - keep this three-part structure; never simply quote the policy.
2. **Cite the applicable clause ID** (for example P-03) so the customer sees the basis and internal review is easier.
3. **Do not promise compensation beyond policy.** If the customer asks for something outside the policy (for example
   extra compensation), flag it for supervisor discretion rather than agreeing to it yourself.
4. **Do not deflect blame** - avoid phrases like "that is the courier's problem" or "the system just works that way".
5. Every reply must state: the applicable clause, what the company will do, what the customer needs to do, and the
   expected completion time.
6. If the customer has clearly expressed dissatisfaction or mentioned a public complaint, add a closing note that the
   case has been escalated to a customer service supervisor.

## 4. Internal notifications when required

- Level A cases: notify both the customer service supervisor and the channel owner.
- Cases involving a store: notify the store manager with the resolution, so the store and customer service do not give
  conflicting answers.
- Three or more defect complaints on the same SKU within 30 days: notify merchandising and quality assurance to assess
  whether the batch should be pulled.
