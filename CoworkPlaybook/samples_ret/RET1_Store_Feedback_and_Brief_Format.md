# Daily Store Feedback and Operations Brief Format

> Fictional training sample; the retail brand, stores and names are placeholders.

## 2. Anomaly rules (apply these thresholds strictly)

1. **Severely behind**: daily achievement rate below 80%.
2. **Behind**: daily achievement rate between 80% and 95%.
3. **Traffic-mix shift**: traffic up more than 20% versus the prior 4-week same-day average, while the average ticket is
   more than 15% below the chain average.
4. **Return anomaly**: an ecommerce channel return rate (returns / orders) above 4%.
5. **Follow-up item**: any store feedback containing keywords such as "equipment", "out of stock" or "construction"
   must be listed as a follow-up item with a named owner.

> Achievement rate = daily sales / daily target. Average ticket = daily sales / traffic.

## 3. Operations brief format (posted to the Teams operations channel)

1. **One-line summary**: whether the chain hit target today, and by how much.
2. **Omnichannel roll-up**: store subtotal, ecommerce subtotal, grand total and overall achievement rate.
3. **Top three things to act on now**: sorted by financial impact, each naming the store/channel and a recommended action.
4. **Achievement rate by store**: sorted ascending, with severely-behind stores flagged.
5. **Follow-up items**: mapped to the store feedback, each with a named owner.

## 4. Tone requirements

- The readers are operations managers and area supervisors: be direct, avoid embellishment and marketing language.
- Every conclusion must tie back to a specific number. Never write vague statements like "performed well".
- Where store feedback explains a numeric anomaly (for example construction blocking the entrance), state that in the
  same line as the store's figures rather than listing numbers alone.
