# Prior-Quarter Incident Record (with resident accessibility assessment)

Last quarter's incident counts were: counter 14, phone 21, online 10, mobile outreach 5 — slightly lower than this quarter, with a broadly flat trend.

## Resident Accessibility Assessment Summary

The mobile outreach unit visits 6 remote stops monthly, serving roughly 1,400 residents; these residents have no broadband access and live more than a 40-minute drive from the nearest physical site, so <b>the mobile unit is their only available channel</b>. The other three channels (counter, phone, online) can substitute for one another — if any one is down, residents can still use another to complete a request.
