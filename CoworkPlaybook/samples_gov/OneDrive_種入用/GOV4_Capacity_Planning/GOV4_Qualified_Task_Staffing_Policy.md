# Qualified-Task Staffing Policy

For any interval flagged as requiring identity-verification-qualified staff, <b>at least one qualified staff member must be present</b>. An unqualified staff member cannot substitute, and “total daily hours meet the requirement” is not a valid substitute reason — this policy is assessed <b>per interval</b>, not by daily total.<br>A hand-off gap with nobody covering counts as zero qualified coverage for that interval, and must be corrected before the service day (by adjusting the roster, extending the hand-off, or adding staff).
