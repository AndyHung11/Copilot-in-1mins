# Reimbursement Rules & Category Mapping

> For Copilot Cowork to reference when consolidating receipts and vouchers.

## Category Mapping & Per-Item Caps
| Category | Documentation Required | Per-Item Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Transport | Invoice or e-ticket detail required | No fixed cap; must match the travel request form |
| Lodging | Invoice required | NT$3,000 per night |
| Meal allowance | May be claimed via voucher, no invoice required | NT$400 per day |
| Office supplies | Invoice required | NT$2,000 per item |

## Reimbursement Deadline
- Documentation must be submitted within 30 days of the expense; late submissions require a memo explaining the delay.

## Reminder Rules
- "Receipt missing": remind the requester to submit the invoice within 3 business days, or reclassify as a voucher-only claim (meal allowance only).
- "Voucher only": if the category is not meal allowance, the requester must submit an invoice — a voucher alone cannot support transport, lodging or office-supply claims.
