# Strategic Account Research Deck Outline — Zava

> This outline defines what the deck should look like.
> **Important**: have Cowork run Enterprise Search across your organisation first, then fill the
> external gaps online, and only then build this deck.

## Deck Goal
Give the internal team a complete picture of Zava as a strategic account: what the company has
already accumulated, what has happened externally, and what we should do next.

## Two Layers of Source

| Layer | Where it comes from | Typical content |
|-------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Internal** | Enterprise Search (SharePoint / OneDrive / Teams / mail) | Meeting notes, quotes, email threads, project docs, colleagues' observations |
| **External** | Deep Research (public web) | Earnings, news, industry trends, competitive landscape |

> **Every slide must state which layer its content came from.** That is what determines
> which points can be said to the client and which stay internal.

## Slide Structure (8 suggested)
1. **Cover**: client name, date, our team
2. **What we already know**: relationship history and key facts from internal material — (internal)
3. **Client update**: company developments, industry trends, recent events — (external)
4. **Challenges they face**: three pain points relevant to our solution — (both)
5. **Collaboration review**: what we delivered and the metrics achieved (placeholder if none)
6. **Value and results**: quantified benefits (cost saved, efficiency gained) mapped to pain points
7. **Recommended next steps**: three opportunities, prioritised
8. **Action items**: named owners and dates

## Style Requirements
- Business-professional and concise; no more than five points per slide.
- Write from the client's perspective ("you" rather than "our product").
- Data first, argument second.
- Any figure you are not certain about **must be a placeholder** — never invent one.

## A Note on Judgement
Internal material often contains lines like "the client complained the rollout takes too long"
or "the contact was non-committal". Those belong in **your own brief**, but they
**must not appear on a slide the client will see**. Ask Cowork to check for this after building.
