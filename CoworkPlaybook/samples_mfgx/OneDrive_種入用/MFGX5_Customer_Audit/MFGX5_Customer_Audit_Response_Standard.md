# Customer Audit Response Standard

**Document**: QP-QA-031　**Revision**: Rev.C　**Effective**: 2026-02-01

## 1 Deadlines

- The corrective action plan (CAP) shall be submitted within
  **10 working days** of receiving the audit report.
- For findings graded **Major**, an interim action shall be provided within
  **3 working days** and shall not be deferred into the permanent
  action.

## 2 ⚠️ Merging rule

**Findings determined to share a single root cause shall be merged into one CAP**,
with every finding number covered stated in the header.

Submitting findings that share a root cause as separate CAPs is treated as a **failure
to identify the root cause**. The customer will return the entire submission for
resubmission, and the number of rejections is recorded in the supplier scorecard.

Conversely, findings with different root causes shall not be merged for convenience.
Improper merging is likewise treated as analysis not performed.

## 3 Required CAP fields

Each CAP shall contain:

1. Finding number (all numbers, where merged)
2. Finding description
3. **Root cause** — traced to the system or process level. "Operator error" alone is
   not acceptable.
4. Interim action, with completion date
5. Permanent action, with completion date
6. Responsible department and owner
7. Verification method and verification date

## 4 Writing requirements

- Actions shall be specific enough to be verified by a third party.
- Statements such as "reinforce awareness", "continuous improvement" or "staff to take
  care" are not accepted, as they cannot be verified.
- Where the root cause is a gap in a procedure, the permanent action shall name
  **which clause of which document** will be revised.

## 5 Tracking

After submission, Quality Assurance tracks the CAP monthly until every action is
complete and verified.
