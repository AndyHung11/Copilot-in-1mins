# Engineering Change Control Procedure

**Document**: QP-ENG-002　**Revision**: Rev.H　**Effective**: 2026-03-01

## 1 Purpose

To govern the issue, review, effectivity and document revision of engineering change
notices (ECNs), so that manufacturing conditions after a change remain consistent with
customer requirements.

## 2 Scope

Applies to changes in product specification, process parameters, work instructions,
packaging and marking.

## 3 Process

### 3.1 Initiation

The originator completes an ECN request stating the change, the reason, the affected
scope and a proposed effective date.

### 3.2 Review

Convened by Product Engineering, with Quality Assurance, Manufacturing, Equipment
Engineering and Document Control. Where customer specifications are affected, written
customer approval shall be obtained first.

### 3.3 Approval

On successful review, the Quality Assurance Manager approves and Document Control
issues and numbers the ECN.

### 3.4 Effectivity

**From the ECN effective date, the new revision governs.**

### 3.5 Document revision

Document Control revises the affected work instructions, parameter sheets and
inspection standards in line with the ECN, and updates the document list.

### 3.6 Training

Manufacturing arranges operator training after documents are revised, and retains the
attendance record.

## 4 Document control

- Only controlled copies may be used on the floor, stamped as controlled.
- The document list is maintained by Document Control and reviewed quarterly.

## 5 Records

ECN requests, review records and customer approvals are retained for five years.

## 6 Responsibilities

| Function | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product Engineering | Initiate and convene the review |
| Quality Assurance | Approve; verify execution |
| Document Control | Issue, revise, maintain the document list |
| Manufacturing | Execute the change; arrange training |
| Equipment Engineering | Adjust equipment conditions accordingly |
