# Engineering Sample Release Criteria

**Document**: QP-QA-019　**Revision**: Rev.E　**Effective**: 2026-04-15

## Order of assessment

Work through the five criteria below. If any one fails, the shipment is not released.

### 1　Qualification status "Qualified"

Release through the normal shipping process, with no additional restriction.

### 2　Qualification status "In qualification"

May ship only as an engineering sample, and must also satisfy:

- A **quantity cap of 50 units per shipment**. Anything above must be split or
  reduced.
- Both the outer carton **and** each individual package must be marked:

  ```
  ENGINEERING SAMPLE — NOT FOR PRODUCTION
  ```

  Marking the carton only, without marking individual packages, counts as incomplete
  marking.

### 3　Where a specification deviation applies

**Both** of the following must hold. **Failing either one blocks release**:

1. Written customer approval has been obtained; and
2. The deviation is **still within its validity period**.

⚠️ That the customer once approved it does not mean the deviation is still valid.
These are two separate conditions and must be checked separately.

### 4　Where the deviation has expired

An extension must be requested from the customer and countersigned before release.
Shipping during the extension request on the grounds that "the customer previously
approved it" is not permitted.

### 5　NPI first sample shipments

In addition to the above, a **first article inspection (FAI) report** must be attached.
Without an FAI there is no release, regardless of customer pressure for material.

## Verdict categories

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Release | Every condition is satisfied |
| Do not release | A condition fails and cannot be corrected before shipment |
| Release with conditions | The condition can be corrected before shipment (reduce quantity, add marking, attach the report) |

## Records

Each assessment shall record the assessor, the date and the criterion applied, retained
for three years.
