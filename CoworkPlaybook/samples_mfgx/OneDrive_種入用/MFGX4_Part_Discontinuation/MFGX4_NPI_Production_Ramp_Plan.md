## 2 Projected critical material usage after MP

| Part | Description | Monthly usage after MP | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| BW-25AU | 25 µm gold wire | **155 spools** | Same diameter as FB-820; shared part number |
| SB-CU10 | 10-layer substrate | 3,600 pcs | New part, sourced separately |
| LF-QFN48 | QFN48 leadframe | 5,200 ea | New part, sourced separately |

⚠️ **BW-25AU is a part number shared with devices already in production.**
After MP, this device's usage must be **added to** FB-820
(52 spools/month) and FB-940 (36 spools/month),
giving a combined **243 spools/month**.

## 3 Capacity planning assumptions

- FB-820 and FB-940 **will not be reduced** when this device enters MP; both hold
  their current monthly output.
- Until full capacity is reached, this device's usage ramps linearly month by month.
  The table above is the steady-state figure at full capacity.

## 4 Material readiness responsibility

Purchasing shall complete an inventory review of shared part numbers **three months
before** the MP date, confirming that combined usage across existing and new devices
will not cause a shortage.
