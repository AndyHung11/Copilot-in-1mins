# Customer Quarterly Business Review (QBR) Deck Standard

**Customer**: Fabrikam Semiconductor
**Document**: QP-CQ-008　**Revision**: Rev.D　**Effective**: 2026-04-01

> This standard is specified by the customer. The section order and content
> requirements may not be altered unilaterally.

## Required sections

### Section 1　Quarter summary

One page covering shipped volume, overall yield and significant events. No more than
five bullets.

### Section 2　Yield trend and comparison with last quarter

- List this quarter and the previous two by device.
- Any device down **more than 1.0 percentage point** quarter on quarter must be
  explained separately, with the action being taken.

### Section 3　Complaints and closure rate

- List every complaint this quarter: case number, date opened, failure mode, status.
- Disclose the closure rate.
- ⚠️ **In addition to the closure rate, the number of repeating failure modes and the
  corresponding case numbers must also be disclosed.** Reporting the closure rate alone
  without disclosing repeats is treated as incomplete, and this section is returned.

### Section 4　8D summaries for significant cases

- Select the most significant cases of the quarter.
- Each must state the D4 root cause and the **D7 horizontal deployment scope**.
- The D7 scope must be stated as it is. Wording such as "continuous improvement in
  progress" is not acceptable.

### Section 5　Improvement commitments and next-quarter targets

Quantifiable commitments with an owner and a due date.

### Section 6　Risk forecast

Proactively disclose items that have not yet produced a complaint but already carry
risk. This is the section the customer weighs most heavily; leaving it blank is treated
as a failure to disclose.

## Presentation rules

- Figures must match the monthly report. Selective reporting is not permitted.
- A recurring problem may not be characterised as an isolated case.
