Dear Valued Customer,

Kinuya Corporation is issuing this Product Change Notification to inform you of the
planned discontinuation of the following bonding wire product.

**Affected part**: BW-25AU (25 µm gold bonding wire, 99.99%)
**Reason**: Consolidation of our fine-wire drawing lines at the Kumamoto facility.
The 25 µm line will cease operation following the transition.

**Key dates**

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| PCN issue date | 2026-09-19 |
| **Last time buy (final PO acceptance)** | **2026-11-30** |
| **Last shipment** | **2027-02-28** |

Orders received after the last time buy date cannot be accepted. Shipments will be
scheduled on a first-come basis within available capacity.

**Recommended replacement**: BW-25AU-N

BW-25AU-N is manufactured on our new line and is dimensionally equivalent, but
differs in elongation and breaking load characteristics. **Requalification is required.**
Based on similar transitions, customers should allow approximately
**4 months** for qualification including reliability testing.

Please confirm your last time buy quantity in writing before the deadline above.
Samples of BW-25AU-N are available on request.

Best regards,

M. Ogawa
Sales Engineering, Kinuya Corporation
