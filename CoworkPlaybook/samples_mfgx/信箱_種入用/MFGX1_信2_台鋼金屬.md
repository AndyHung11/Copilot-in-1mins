## 中文版

**主旨**：【台鋼金屬】MT-3305 鍍層厚度檢測結果通知

**內文**：

品保部 您好：

我司 8/19 出貨之 TS26081901 批次，事後複測發現鍍層平均厚度為 42μm，低於我們自訂的 50μm 內控值。

由於產線交期壓力，該批已先行出貨。我司認為對外觀與初期防鏽影響有限，但仍主動向貴司說明，如需更換我司可安排回收。

台鋼金屬 業務部 黃士豪

## English

**Subject**: [TaiSteel Metal] MT-3305 coating thickness test results

**Body**:

Dear QA team,

For lot TS26081901 shipped on 8/19, our follow-up re-measurement found an average coating thickness of 42μm, below our internal control value of 50μm.

Because of line schedule pressure the lot had already shipped. We believe the impact on appearance and early-stage rust resistance is limited, but we are informing you proactively; we can arrange a recall if replacement is needed.

Shih-Hao Huang, Sales, TaiSteel Metal
