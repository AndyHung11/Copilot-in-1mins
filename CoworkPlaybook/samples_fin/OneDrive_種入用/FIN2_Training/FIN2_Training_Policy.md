# Compliance Training Policy

Version: 2026 ｜ Maintained by: Group Compliance Office

---

## Article 1 — Purpose

To ensure that everyone at Zava Financial Holdings and its subsidiaries holds the compliance knowledge
their role requires.

## Article 2 — Annual training and the completion rate

1. The group runs one annual compliance training programme. The Group Compliance Office
   publishes the theme at the start of each year.
2. **The completion rate shall be no lower than 90%.**
3. The completion rate is measured **by unit**. The group total **and** every unit must
   meet the threshold.

## Article 3 — Calculating the completion rate

```
completion rate = completed ÷ population required to train × 100%
```

The **population required to train** is headcount at the reporting date, less those
excluded under Article 4.

## Article 4 — Excluding new hires

1. Anyone with **less than three months' service** at the reporting date takes
   induction compliance training under the Induction Training Procedure instead, and
   **is not counted** in the annual training population.
2. Those people are **removed from the denominator**. They are not to be recorded as
   having completed.
3. Human Resources supplies the exclusion list; Compliance reconciles it.

> ⚠️ The two treatments give different answers. Removing people from the denominator
> **raises** the completion rate; recording them as complete raises numerator and
> denominator together. This policy takes the former.

## Article 5 — Level of breakdown

1. The completion rate shall be reported at **subsidiary** level as a minimum.
2. Where a subsidiary's population differs materially in the nature of the work,
   it shall be **broken down further by population type**. A subsidiary-level total is
   not sufficient to establish compliance.
3. **Field sales agents in the insurance business** face customers directly, are
   numerous and turn over frequently, and shall therefore be **reported separately**.

> Articles 5-2 and 5-3 are the ones most often passed over in practice: a subsidiary
> total above the threshold does not mean every population type within it is.

## Article 6 — Where a unit falls short

1. The Group Compliance Office writes to the Head of Compliance of any unit below
   threshold, requiring completion within one month.
2. Where the shortfall sits in a population central to the regulator's assessment, a
   remediation plan shall be tabled at the following month's compliance meeting.

## Article 7 — Reporting date

The reporting date for this year is **2026-09-20**.
