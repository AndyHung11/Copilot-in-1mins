# Supervisory Bureau Matrix

Version: 2026 ｜ Maintained by: Group Compliance Office
Use: deciding which regulator receives incident reports, routine filings and correspondence

---

## The matrix

| Entity | Nature of business | Supervisory bureau |
|---|---|---|
| Zava Financial Holdings (parent) | Financial holding | Banking Bureau |
| Zava Commercial Bank | Banking | Banking Bureau |
| Zava Life | Life insurance | Insurance Bureau |
| Zava Securities | Securities brokerage, proprietary trading, underwriting | **Securities and Futures Bureau** |
| Zava Investment Trust | Securities investment trust | **Securities and Futures Bureau** |

## Common errors

| Error | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| Sending everything to the Banking Bureau | The holding company sits with the Banking Bureau, but **each subsidiary has its own bureau**. The parent's does not carry down |
| Deciding by where the entity is located | Location is irrelevant. It follows the **nature of the business** |
| Treating securities and the investment trust as one | Both answer to the Securities and Futures Bureau, but **each reports for itself**. They cannot be combined into one report |

## How to decide

1. **Which entity** did this happen in?
2. What is that entity's **line of business**?
3. Read the bureau off the matrix.

> Where the incident occurred in a subsidiary, the reporting entity is **that
> subsidiary**. Group Compliance supervises and assists, but does not report in its place.
