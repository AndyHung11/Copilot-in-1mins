# Compliance Sign-off Turnaround Rules

Version: 2026 ｜ Maintained by: Group Compliance Office

---

## 1. Scope

New product submissions sent by subsidiaries to the Group Compliance Office for sign-off.

## 2. Turnaround

| Route | What Compliance must do | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| **Approval** cases | Issue a compliance opinion | Within **3 business days**, counted from the **business day after receipt** |
| **Filing** cases | **No opinion required.** File and close | No deadline, but file the same day |

## 3. Counting, and rolling over

1. **The day of receipt does not count.** The clock starts the next business day.
2. Public holidays, weekends and days on which financial institutions do not open
   **do not count**, and the deadline rolls forward accordingly.
3. Receipt time is when the mail reached the **Compliance Office shared mailbox** —
   not the date the submitting team put on the document.

### Worked example

Received on a Thursday, 3-business-day turnaround:

| Day | Counts as |
|---|---|
| Thursday (day of receipt) | Does not count |
| Friday | Business day 1 |
| Saturday, Sunday | Do not count |
| Monday | Business day 2 |
| Tuesday | Business day 3 → **deadline** |

## 4. Subject-line flags have no bearing on the deadline

Where a submitting team marks a subject line "URGENT", "for immediate action" or
similar, **it changes nothing under these rules** — neither the turnaround nor whether
the case is a filing or an approval.

Compliance determines the route from the Filing vs Approval Matrix, then applies these
rules to get the deadline.

> A note from practice: the cases flagged urgent are frequently **filing** cases — the
> submitting team simply wants them cleared quickly. The approval cases, which are the
> ones actually on a clock, often arrive unflagged.
> **Do not let the subject line set your order of work.**

## 5. Missed deadlines

Where an approval case passes its deadline without an opinion, the Head of Compliance
records the reason and reports it at the next quarterly compliance coordination meeting.
