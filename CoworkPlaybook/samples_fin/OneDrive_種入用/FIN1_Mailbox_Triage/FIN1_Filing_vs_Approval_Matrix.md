# Insurance Product Submission Routes — Filing vs Approval

Applies to: Zava Financial Holdings / Zava Life
Version: 2026 ｜ Maintained by: Group Compliance Office

---

## 1. Two routes, and what separates them

Before an insurance product can be sold, it goes to the regulator by one of two routes,
determined by product type:

| Route | Who submits | What Compliance does | Regulator |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Filing** | Product team submits directly | **No compliance opinion required.** File the copy and close it | Reviews after the fact |
| **Approval** | Product team submits, **a compliance opinion must be attached** | **Must issue a compliance opinion**, within the turnaround rules | Approval required before sale |

⚠️ Internally, submissions for both routes are called "product compliance
confirmation". **The subject line does not tell you which is which** — you have to
work it out from the product type.

## 2. Product type matrix

| # | Product type | Route | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Traditional life (whole life, term life) | **Filing** | Fixed benefits; the policyholder selects no investment options |
| 2 | Health insurance (base policy or rider) | **Filing** | Medical, critical illness, long-term care |
| 3 | Personal accident and travel accident | **Filing** | |
| 4 | Annuity — **interest-sensitive** | **Filing** | The declared rate is set by the company; the policyholder selects no investment options |
| 5 | Annuity — **variable** | **Approval** | Investment-linked |
| 6 | **Investment-linked products** (variable life, variable universal life, variable annuity) | **Approval** | The policyholder selects investment options and bears the investment risk |
| 7 | Insurance linked to structured products | **Approval** | |
| 8 | Single rider that does not alter the benefit structure | **Filing** | |

## 3. How to decide

Two questions settle it:

1. **Does the policyholder choose investment options and bear the investment risk?**
   If yes → investment-linked → **approval**.
2. **Does the benefit structure vary?**
   If no → traditional / health / accident → **filing**.

Any product whose name contains "**variable**" is investment-linked, and goes by
approval, without exception.

## 4. Where people get it wrong

| Mistake | What is actually true |
|---|---|
| Reading "interest-sensitive" as investment-linked | An interest-sensitive annuity goes by **filing**. The company declares the rate; the policyholder picks nothing |
| Reading "universal" as filing | **Variable** universal life is investment-linked and goes by **approval**. Plain universal life is filing |
| Going by how the submitting team flagged the subject line | An URGENT flag has nothing to do with the route. See the Turnaround Rules |
