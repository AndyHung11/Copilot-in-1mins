# New Product Compliance Sign-off Checklist

Version: 2026 ｜ Maintained by: Group Compliance Office
**This is the version the submitting business unit sees.**

---

## Attach the following

| # | Checkpoint | Documents to attach | Attached |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Suitability tiering | Risk rating assessment, suitability mapping rules | ☐ |
| 2 | Risk rating disclosure | Where and how the risk rating is displayed | ☐ |
| 3 | Disclosure documents | Product description, risk warning, fee schedule | ☐ |
| 4 | Marketing language | Marketing copy and promotional material, digital included | ☐ |
| 5 | Complaint handling | Complaint categories and handling process | ☐ |

## The submission must state

1. Product name, type and intended launch date
2. **Intended distribution channels** (select all that apply)
3. Target customers and suitability tier
4. Fee structure
5. Risk rating and the basis for it

## What Compliance will do

| Route | Turnaround |
|---|---|
| Approval cases | 3 business days from the day after receipt |
| Filing cases | Filed; no opinion issued |

---

> ⚠️ **This checklist lists the checkpoints only.**
> What each checkpoint actually requires **differs by distribution channel**. See the
> Channel Requirements document.
>
> Business units organise their material **by product** — this is a fund, this is a
> policy. Compliance review runs **by channel**. The same product sold through two
> channels does not need the same pack.
