# Industry Case Library — New Product Launch Findings

Version: September 2026 ｜ Maintained by: Group Compliance Office
8 cases. Use: evidence base when building sign-off criteria.

---

## Case 1　Online subscription with no suitability block

A peer opened fund subscription online without blocking unsuitable subscriptions by
risk profile. Conservative customers could subscribe to high-risk products directly.
**Channel**: Digital (online subscription) ｜ Checkpoint: Suitability tiering

## Case 2　Same product compliant at the counter, non-compliant online

A peer's structured product had a complete branch sales process, but the online channel
offered no Seven-day cooling-off period, leaving customers with no way to unwind after subscribing.
**Channel**: Digital (online subscription) ｜ Checkpoint: Complaint handling

> The classic one: **nothing wrong with the product. The problem was the channel.**

## Case 3　Risk rating only visible after scrolling

In a peer's app subscription flow, the risk rating sat at the foot of the page,
requiring two screens of scrolling on a typical handset.
**Channel**: Digital (online subscription) ｜ Checkpoint: Risk rating disclosure

## Case 4　Social posts not reviewed by Compliance

A peer's sales staff posted product performance from personal social accounts without
compliance review, showing only periods of positive return.
**Channel**: Digital (online subscription) ｜ Checkpoint: Marketing language

## Case 5　Electronic delivery with no record of reading

A peer delivered risk warnings electronically but logged only "sent", retaining no
record that the customer opened or read them.
**Channel**: Digital (online subscription) ｜ Checkpoint: Disclosure documents

## Case 6　Sub-brokerage risk warning not signed

A peer accepted sub-brokerage orders without obtaining a signed warning covering the
trading risks of the specific market.
**Channel**: Securities sub-brokerage ｜ Checkpoint: Suitability tiering

## Case 7　No record of the adviser's suitability check

A peer sold investment-linked policies at the counter where the adviser confirmed
suitability verbally, retaining neither a conversation record nor a written one. Nothing
could be verified afterwards.
**Channel**: Branch (bank wealth management) ｜ Checkpoint: Suitability tiering

## Case 8　Online complaints route with no response time

A peer's digital channel had an online complaints route but published no response time.
Average days to respond ran far longer than at the branch.
**Channel**: Digital (online subscription) ｜ Checkpoint: Complaint handling
