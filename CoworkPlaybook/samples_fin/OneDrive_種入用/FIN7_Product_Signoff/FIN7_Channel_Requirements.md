# Channel Requirements

Version: 2026 ｜ Maintained by: Group Compliance Office
**This is the Compliance review version — the real basis for sign-off.**

---

## 1. Why the criteria hang on the channel

The same product gives the customer **different protection** depending on where it is sold:

| Channel | Someone explains it in person | Customer can ask there and then | Chance to stop and think |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branch (bank wealth management) | Yes, an adviser | Yes | Depends on the adviser |
| **Digital (online subscription)** | **No** | **No** | **Must be built into the process** |
| Securities sub-brokerage | Yes, a dealer | Yes | Depends on the dealer |

Where nobody explains anything, **additional steps** have to make up the difference.
That is why the digital channel carries several requirements the others do not.

## 2. Requirement by requirement

### Checkpoint 1: Suitability tiering

| Channel | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Branch (bank wealth management) | Adviser compares manually against the KYC result; conversation record retained |
| Digital (online subscription) | **The system blocks** unsuitable subscriptions; an **Online risk tolerance test** is required and must be within validity |
| Securities sub-brokerage | Dealer compares; sub-brokerage additionally requires a signed risk warning |

### Checkpoint 2: Risk rating disclosure

| Channel | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Branch (bank wealth management) | On the first page of the paper documentation |
| Digital (online subscription) | On **every page** of the subscription flow, and visible without scrolling |
| Securities sub-brokerage | On the order entry screen |

### Checkpoint 3: Disclosure documents

| Channel | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Branch (bank wealth management) | Paper delivered and receipt acknowledged |
| Digital (online subscription) | Electronic delivery with a **retained record that it was opened**; an **Electronic signature verification** is also required |
| Securities sub-brokerage | Electronic or paper delivery with acknowledgement |

### Checkpoint 4: Marketing language

| Channel | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Branch (bank wealth management) | Material reviewed by Compliance |
| Digital (online subscription) | Material, **in-app notifications, social posts and search advertising** all reviewed |
| Securities sub-brokerage | Material reviewed by Compliance |

### Checkpoint 5: Complaint handling

| Channel | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Branch (bank wealth management) | Taken at the branch, handled under the complaints process |
| Digital (online subscription) | An **online complaints route** with a published response time; a route to invoke the **Seven-day cooling-off period** is also required |
| Securities sub-brokerage | Taken at the dealing office |

## 3. ⭐ The three that only the digital channel needs

These apply to **Digital (online subscription) only**. Neither of the other two channels needs them:

| # | Requirement | Why only digital |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Online risk tolerance test** | No adviser confirms risk profile face to face, so the system has to |
| 2 | **Seven-day cooling-off period** | Nothing was explained in person, so the customer needs time to reconsider |
| 3 | **Electronic signature verification** | No paper signature, so intent has to be evidenced electronically |

> These three are the ones that disappear entirely when criteria are built by product
> class — because they have nothing to do with what the product is, and everything to do
> with **how it is sold**.

## 4. Reaching a conclusion

- All three channels fully covered → pass
- Gaps that can be remedied → pass with conditions, listing what is outstanding **and
  which channel requires it**
- Gaps in a core customer-protection step → reject

The conclusion must **state which channel requires each outstanding item**. Otherwise
the submitting unit has no way of knowing what to supply.
