# Information Barrier Rules

Version: 2026 ｜ Maintained by: Group Compliance Office
Applies to: Zava Financial Holdings and its subsidiaries

---

## Article 1 — Purpose

Zava Securities conducts proprietary trading, underwriting and brokerage; Zava Investment Trust
manages funds. Where information from both sides reaches the same person, that is a
conflict of interest and may prejudice fund beneficiaries. These rules exist to prevent
it.

## Article 2 — Information within scope

The following **may not reach the same person**:

| Class | Content |
|---|---|
| A | Zava Investment Trust **intended holdings, planned position changes, and unusual subscription or redemption** activity in the funds it manages |
| B | Zava Securities **proprietary position plans, non-public underwriting information, and research not yet published** |
| C | Zava Investment Trust fund **channel allocation, channel incentive terms, and channel-level sales forecasts** |

## Article 3 — People holding concurrent roles

1. Where a smaller subsidiary's Head of Compliance role is held **concurrently** by the
   Head of Compliance of a closely related subsidiary, that is a legitimate staffing
   arrangement and these rules do not prohibit it.
2. However, where such a person would be present for a meeting, document or discussion
   that engages Class A, B or C information, one of the following applies:

   | Approach | What it means | When to use it |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Separate session** | Take the item **out of the main meeting** and hold it separately, with only that subsidiary's people present | ⭐ Where the item must be discussed and the person holding both roles is a necessary attendee for that subsidiary |
   | Recusal | The person leaves the room for that item | Where they are not a necessary attendee for that item |
   | Written submission | Written comments instead of attendance | Where the item is straightforward and needs no live discussion |

3. An undertaking by the individual not to disclose **is not** an acceptable substitute.

## Article 4 — Checks when scheduling

Before invitations go out, the convening function shall:

- [ ] Check the `Compliance_Contact_Roster.md` for concurrent roles
- [ ] Go through the agenda item by item and mark those within scope
- [ ] Choose an approach under Article 3 for each item in scope
- [ ] Where a separate session is used, **confirm the attendee list for each session
      independently**

## Article 5 — Records

Where separate sessions are held, minutes shall be produced separately and shall not be
combined. The reason for separating them shall be recorded.
