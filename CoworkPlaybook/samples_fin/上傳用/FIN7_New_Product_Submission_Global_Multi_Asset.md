## 5. How the digital channel works

The online subscription flow:

1. Customer signs in to internet banking or the app
2. Views the fund page, which displays the risk rating
3. Selects a subscription amount
4. Confirms fees and the risk warning (delivered electronically, opening recorded)
5. Confirms by Electronic signature verification
6. Debit and execution

> The customer's risk profile is carried over from the data captured when the account
> was opened at a branch.
