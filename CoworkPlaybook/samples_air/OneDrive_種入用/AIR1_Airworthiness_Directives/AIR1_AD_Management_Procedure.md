# AD Management Procedure (extract)

> Document owner: Engineering · Technical Services　|　ZV-ENG-P-0104　|　Revision 3.1

## 1. Scope

This procedure governs compliance-deadline management for Airworthiness Directives
(ADs) on aircraft operated by Zava Airlines. ADs are mandatory requirements issued or
adopted by the CAA. **An aircraft with an overdue AD may not be dispatched**, and no
extension or alternative means is available.

## 2. Compliance basis

An AD deadline is expressed in one of three bases, **specified by the issuing
authority. Operators may not convert between them or substitute one for another**:

| Basis | Unit | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Calendar days | days | Counted from the effective date, regardless of whether the aircraft flies |
| Flight hours | FH | Counted from the accumulated flight hours at the effective date |
| Flight cycles | FC | Counted from the accumulated flight cycles at the effective date |

⚠️ **Remaining quantities on different bases cannot be compared directly.** Flight
hours and flight cycles must first be converted to estimated days remaining, using
that aircraft's own average utilisation over the past 30 days, before they can be
ranked alongside calendar-day items.

Conversion:
```
Estimated days remaining = remaining quantity / that aircraft's average daily utilisation over 30 days
```

Utilisation differs from tail to tail and shifts with hangar visits and seasonal
schedule changes. **Pull the current figures for every assessment; never reuse the
numbers from the previous review.**

## 3. Warning thresholds

| Estimated days remaining | Action |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 60 | Added to the daily watch list with a named responsible engineer |
| Fewer than 30 | A compliance plan is required, with man-hours and parts confirmed with Maintenance Planning |
| Fewer than 14 | Escalate to the Head of Engineering; adjust the flight schedule if necessary |

## 4. Common misjudgements

1. **Picking whichever number looks smallest.** Whether 300 remaining cycles bites
   before 500 remaining flight hours depends entirely on that aircraft's utilisation
   mix, not on the size of the number.
2. **Using one utilisation figure for every AD on a tail.** Flight-hour items need
   the FH daily average; flight-cycle items need the FC daily average. They are not
   interchangeable.
3. **Using an average that covers a hangar visit.** An aircraft does not fly while it
   is in the hangar, which depresses the 30-day average and badly overstates the
   days remaining.

## 5. Prohibited

- Do not remove an item from the watch list on the grounds that the aircraft is
  "going into the hangar soon".
- Do not re-express a calendar-day requirement in flight hours.
- Do not change a compliance deadline without approval from the CAA.
